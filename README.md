MyApplication is a simple Android app that encourages small, low effort acts of social connection. You enter a time of day, and the app suggests a quick, specific gesture to reach out to someone, such as texting a family member good morning, thanking a colleague, or calling a friend for a quick catch up. The goal is to make staying connected easy by removing the guesswork of what to say and when.
The app is built with Kotlin in Android Studio. It’s intentionally minimal: a single screen, no accounts, and nothing saved between sessions. As you type a time of day, a short autocomplete list helps guide you to a valid option, and if what you type doesn’t match anything recognized, the app shows a friendly message listing the valid choices rather than a plain error.
The project is hosted on GitHub, where commits track the history of changes to the code.
